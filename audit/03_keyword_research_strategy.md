## 2. Competitive Content Gap Analysis

Competitor articles from generic institutes typically suffer from:
1. **Generic textbook definitions:** Repeating Wikipedia-style descriptions without showing real transaction codes (OBYC, SPRO, BP, FAGLL03).
2. **Lack of real business scenarios:** Failing to explain *why* an error occurs (e.g. why MIGO fails with error `M8 147` or `F5 702`).
3. **Absence of mentor connection:** No direct channel to ask questions or verify real configuration.

**ERP Academy's Competitive Advantage:**
- Every blog post provides concrete transaction codes, field-level configuration paths, real accounting entries, and direct mentor WhatsApp CTA.
- FAQ schema generates rich snippets in search results.
- `llms.txt` optimizes for generative AI citations.
