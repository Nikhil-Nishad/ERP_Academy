# ERP Academy — 30, 60 & 90-Day Strategic Growth Roadmap

This roadmap sets concrete, measurable milestones for scaling organic search visibility, AI search citations (GEO), and student course inquiries.

---

## 📅 Phase 1: Days 1–30 (Foundation, Indexing & Initial Traction)

### Primary Objectives
- Achieve 100% search engine indexation of all 39 technical blogs.
- Activate Google Analytics 4 tracking verification in production.
- Capture initial ranking gains in Google Search Console positions 4–15.
- Generate first 30+ qualified direct WhatsApp / Demo booking inquiries.

### Key Deliverables & Action Items
1. **Indexing & Recrawling:**
   - Submit sitemap `https://erp-academy.vercel.app/sitemap.xml` directly in Google Search Console.
   - Use URL Inspection tool to submit new high-priority blogs:
     - `/blog/sap-s4hana-vs-ecc-differences`
     - `/blog/sap-mm-fi-integration-obyc-guide`
     - `/blog/sap-certification-cost-in-india`
     - `/blog/non-it-to-sap-consultant-transition`
   - Re-ping IndexNow after any new blog updates.
2. **Analytics & Conversion Verification:**
   - Confirm `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID` is active in Vercel project environment variables.
   - Set up custom GA4 conversion events for `whatsapp_click` and `demo_booking_submit`.
3. **Internal Linking Enhancement:**
   - Add contextual inline links from older high-traffic blogs (`/blog/sap-mm-tutorial-beginners`, `/blog/sap-fico-course-roadmap-career-guide`) pointing directly to the new in-depth guides.

## 📅 Phase 2: Days 31–60 (Authority Building, GEO & Expansion)

### Primary Objectives
- Grow monthly organic impressions to 50,000+ across target SAP queries.

- Expand conversion rate from 2.5% to 4.5% on blog traffic.

### Key Deliverables & Action Items
1. **GEO / AI Search Optimization:**
   - Maintain and expand `public/llms.txt` with clear, structured summaries of each module, curriculum details, and trainer credentials.
   - Ensure FAQ sections directly answer the top conversational queries asked in AI search engines.
2. **Course Landing Page Optimization:**
   - Add student video testimonials or career transition case studies to `/courses/sap-mm` and `/courses/sap-fi`.
   - Implement clear syllabus download lead magnets (e.g. "Download SAP MM 2026 S/4HANA Curriculum PDF" capturing name, phone, email).
3. **Content Expansion:**
   - Publish 4 additional scenario-based interview guides targeting high-intent long-tail keywords (e.g. "SAP MM Release Strategy configuration", "SAP FICO Month-End Closing checklist").
