## What is SAP Materials Management (MM)?

SAP MM is a core functional component of the SAP ERP and SAP S/4HANA enterprise suite. It oversees all business processes related to:

1. **Procurement & Purchasing:** Sourcing goods, issuing RFQs, managing Purchase Orders (PO), and negotiating contracts.
2. **Inventory Management:** Monitoring stock levels, tracking goods receipts (`MIGO`), transfers, and physical inventory.
3. **Master Data:** Maintaining central databases for Material Masters, Vendor / Business Partners, and Purchasing Info Records (`PIR`).
4. **Invoice Verification:** Reconciling vendor invoices (`MIRO`) against Purchase Orders and Goods Receipts (3-way match).
5. **Valuation & Account Assignment:** Automatic G/L posting integration with SAP FI via `OBYC` configuration.
