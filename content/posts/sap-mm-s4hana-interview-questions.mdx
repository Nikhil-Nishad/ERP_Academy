# Top 35 SAP MM & S/4HANA Sourcing Interview Questions & Answers (Scenario-Based)

Whether you are interviewing for an SAP MM Associate Consultant opening at Infosys, a Senior Consultant role at Deloitte, or an internal support analyst position at a multinational corporation, one thing is certain:

> **Modern technical interviewers do NOT ask simple theoretical definitions like "What is a purchase order?" They test you with real-time enterprise scenarios, configuration pitfalls, and S/4HANA changes.**

To help you clear your technical rounds with confidence, mentor **Akshay Kumar** and the senior consulting team at ERP Academy have compiled the **top 35 scenario-based interview questions** with detailed, technically precise model answers.

## Category 1: Enterprise Structure & Master Data (S/4HANA)

### Q1: What happens to transaction codes XK01, MK01, and FK01 in SAP S/4HANA?
**Answer:** In SAP S/4HANA, classic vendor master transactions (`XK01`, `MK01`, `FK01`) and customer master transactions (`XD01`, `VD01`, `FD01`) are obsolete and permanently redirected to centralized transaction **`BP` (Business Partner)**. 
Through Customer-Vendor Integration (CVI), every vendor or customer is created as a Business Partner. To create an MM vendor:
1. Create a BP with general data (Category: Organization).
2. Assign role **`FLVN00`** for Financial Accounting vendor view (Company Code data).
3. Assign role **`FLVN01`** for Purchasing vendor view (Purchasing Organization data).

---

### Q2: Can a single Plant be assigned to multiple Company Codes in SAP MM?
**Answer:** **No.** In SAP enterprise structure, the relationship between Plant and Company Code is strictly **Many-to-One ($N:1$)**. Multiple plants can belong to one Company Code, but a single plant can only be assigned to exactly one Company Code. However, a Purchasing Organization can be assigned to multiple plants across different Company Codes (Cross-Company Code Purchasing Organization).

---

### Q3: What is the difference between a Purchasing Organization and a Purchasing Group?
**Answer:** 
- **Purchasing Organization:** An organizational unit responsible for procuring materials and services, negotiating pricing conditions with vendors, and legally signing purchase contracts. It can be plant-specific, cross-plant, or cross-company code.
- **Purchasing Group:** An internal operational buyer or team of buyers responsible for day-to-day purchasing activities (creating PRs, POs). It is **not** linked to the enterprise organizational hierarchy in `SPRO` and operates independently across all purchasing organizations.

---

### Q4: Explain the significance of the "Account Category Reference" in Material Master.
**Answer:** The Account Category Reference (ACR) acts as a bridge between Material Types and Valuation Classes. You link Material Types (e.g., `ROH`, `FERT`) to an ACR, and then link Valuation Classes (e.g., `3000`, `7920`) to that ACR. This ensures that when a user creates a raw material (`ROH`) in `MM01`, the system only permits them to select valid Raw Material Valuation Classes, preventing severe accounting errors.

---

### Q5: How is Material Master deleted in SAP? Can you permanently drop it from the database?
**Answer:** In SAP standard, you cannot directly drop a material record from database tables like `MARA` or `MARC`. You must set a **Deletion Flag** using transaction `MM06` at the client, plant, or storage location level. Once flagged, the material can no longer be used in new purchase orders or sales orders. Permanent removal requires executing an **Archiving Run** (`SARA` / Object `MM_MATNR`).

## Category 2: Procurement Lifecycle & Document Control

### Q6: Walk me through the complete Procure-to-Pay (P2P) cycle and the tables involved.
**Answer:** 
1. **Determination of Requirements:** Purchase Requisition (`ME51N`) $\rightarrow$ Table `EBAN`.
2. **Source Determination:** RFQ (`ME41`), Quotation (`ME47`), Comparison (`ME49`) $\rightarrow$ Tables `EKKO` / `EKPO`.
3. **Vendor Selection & Purchase Order:** PO Creation (`ME21N`) $\rightarrow$ Header `EKKO`, Line item `EKPO`.
4. **Goods Receipt:** Physical receipt (`MIGO`) $\rightarrow$ S/4HANA writes to `MATDOC` (ECC wrote to `MKPF` / `MSEG`).
5. **Invoice Verification:** Accounts Payable match (`MIRO`) $\rightarrow$ Tables `RBKP` (Header) and `RSEG` (Line item).
6. **Payment Run:** Financial settlement (`F110`).

---

### Q7: What is the difference between a Contract and a Scheduling Agreement?
**Answer:** 
- **Contract (`ME31K`):** An outline agreement with a vendor stating total quantity (Quantity Contract - `MK`) or total value (Value Contract - `WK`) over a fixed period without specific delivery dates. Deliveries occur only when a **Contract Release Order** is created.
- **Scheduling Agreement (`ME31L`):** An outline agreement where delivery dates and quantities are scheduled explicitly in **Delivery Schedule Lines** (`ME38`). No separate purchase orders are generated; goods receipts are posted directly against the scheduling agreement.

---

### Q8: What is a Source List and what happens if the "Source List Requirement" is checked in Plant customizing?
**Answer:** A Source List (`ME01`) defines approved, blocked, or preferred supply sources for a material in a plant over specified validity dates. If the **"Source List Requirement"** checkbox is activated for a plant in customizing (`OMET` / `SPRO`), users **cannot** save a Purchase Order without the vendor being explicitly maintained as an approved source in the Source List.

---

### Q9: Scenario: A buyer creates a PO with 100 units. The vendor ships 105 units. How does SAP handle over-deliveries?
**Answer:** SAP checks the **Overdelivery Tolerance Limit** maintained in the PO line item under the "Delivery" tab (derived from the Purchasing Info Record). 
- If over-delivery tolerance is set to $10\%$, `MIGO` will allow receiving up to 110 units.
- If over-delivery is set to $0\%$ and "Unlimited Overdelivery" is unchecked, `MIGO` throws error message: `M7 022: PU Overdelivery tolerance exceeded by 5 units`.

## Category 3: MM-FI Integration & OBYC Account Determination

### Q10: What are the mandatory parameters required for Automatic Account Determination in OBYC?
**Answer:** 
1. Chart of Accounts (`KTOPL`)
2. Valuation Grouping Code (`BWMOD` / Valuation Modification)
3. Transaction / Event Key (`KTOSL` - e.g., `BSX`, `WRX`, `PRD`)
4. Account Modifier / General Modification (`KOMOK` - used in `GBB`, `PRD`)
5. Valuation Class (`BKLAS` - from Material Master Accounting 1 view)

---

### Q11: Explain the exact accounting entries for MIGO when receiving standard priced materials at a variance.
**Answer:** 
- Material Standard Price in Master: ₹100 per unit.
- Purchase Order Price: ₹120 per unit.
- Quantity Received in `MIGO`: 10 units.

**Accounting Postings:**
- **Debit:** Stock Account (`BSX`) = $10 \times ₹100 = \mathbf{₹1,000}$ (Always valued at standard price).
- **Debit:** Price Difference Account (`PRD`) = $10 \times ₹20 = \mathbf{₹200}$ (Expense).
- **Credit:** GR/IR Clearing Account (`WRX`) = $10 \times ₹120 = \mathbf{₹1,200}$ (Liability based on PO value).

---

### Q12: What is the role of Transaction Key GBB and its Account Modifiers?
**Answer:** Key `GBB` is the offsetting entry for inventory movements during internal issues, stock adjustments, and scrapping where no purchase order exists. Key account modifiers include:
- **`VBR`:** Consumption for Cost Center / Internal Order (Movement Types `201`, `261`).
- **`VNG`:** Scrapping / Shrinkage expense (Movement Type `551`).
- **`BSA`:** Initial stock upload (Movement Type `561`).
- **`INV`:** Inventory difference adjustments from physical count (`MI07`).

## Category 4: PO Release Strategy (Approval Workflow)

### Q13: What are the key elements required to configure a PO Release Strategy without workflow?
**Answer:** 
1. **Characteristics (`CT04`):** Define fields from communication structure `CEKKO` (e.g., `CEKKO-GNETW` for Total PO Net Value, `CEKKO-BSART` for Document Type, `CEKKO-WERKS` for Plant).
2. **Class (`CL02`):** Create a Release Class under Class Type **`032` (Release Strategy)** and assign the characteristics.
3. **Release Group:** A 2-character code grouping strategies linked to Class `032`.
4. **Release Codes:** Individual approver roles (e.g., `L1` for Manager, `L2` for HOD, `L3` for VP).
5. **Release Indicators:** Set status (e.g., `B` for Blocked, `X` for Released for PO transmission).
6. **Release Prerequisites & Strategies:** Define the approval sequence and monetary thresholds.

---

### Q14: Scenario: An approved PO is modified by changing the delivery date. Will the release strategy re-trigger?
**Answer:** It depends on the **Changeability Indicator** set in the Release Indicator customizing:
- If Changeability is set to `1` or `2`: Cannot be changed after release.
- If Changeability is set to `4` or `6`: Changing values re-triggers the release strategy only if the total net value increases beyond the tolerance percentage configured.
- Changing only the delivery date or text typically does not reset release unless `CEKKO` was extended with date fields.

## Category 5: Special Procurement (Subcontracting & STO)

### Q15: Explain the complete process flow and accounting entries for Subcontracting.
**Answer:** 
1. **BOM & Info Record:** Create a Subcontracting Info Record (`Item Category L`) and a Bill of Materials (`CS01`) for the finished product.
2. **Subcontracting PO (`ME21N`):** Enter Item Category `L`. The system automatically explodes components from the BOM.
3. **Transfer Components to Vendor (`ME2O` or `MIGO - 541`):** Movement `541` transfers raw material to "Stock Provided to Vendor" (No accounting entry generated, as goods remain company asset).
4. **Goods Receipt of Finished Product (`MIGO - 101`):**
   - **Dr:** Finished Good Inventory (`BSX`)
   - **Cr:** Change in Finished Stock (`BSV`)
   - **Dr:** Subcontracting Service Expense (`FRL`)
   - **Cr:** GR/IR Clearing (`WRX`)
   - **Dr:** Raw Material Consumption (`GBB - VBO`)
   - **Cr:** Raw Material Inventory (`BSX`)

---

### Q16: What is a Stock Transport Order (STO) and what are its types?
**Answer:** An STO is used to transfer inventory between plants.
1. **Intra-Company STO (Same Company Code):**
   - Without Delivery: Direct `MIGO` with Movement `301` or 2-step `303` / `305`.
   - With Delivery via Shipping (`SD`): PO Type `UB`, Movement `641` at issue, `101` at receiving plant. No invoice created.
2. **Inter-Company STO (Different Company Codes):**
   - Standard PO Type `NB`, Customer and Vendor created for plants.
   - Movement `643` at issuing plant, internal billing (`IV`) via `VF01`, and `101` + `MIRO` at receiving plant.

## Quick-Fire Interview Questions (Q17–Q35)

- **Q17: What is Split Valuation?** When identical materials are valued differently based on origin (Domestic vs Imported) or status (New vs Refurbished). Configured in `OMWC`.
- **Q18: What is the difference between Gross Price and Net Price in a PO?** Gross price excludes discounts, surcharges, and taxes. Net price includes discounts and additions.
- **Q19: What is the function of the Evaluated Receipt Settlement (ERS)?** Allows automatic invoice generation upon goods receipt without the vendor submitting a physical paper invoice (T-Code `MRRL`).
- **Q20: What is a Consignment Purchase?** The vendor stores goods on your warehouse floor, but legal ownership remains with the vendor until goods are consumed (Movement `411 K`). Payment is settled via `MRKO`.
- **Q21: What is the table for Purchase Order History?** Table **`EKBE`**.
- **Q22: Which movement type is used for returning goods to vendor against a PO?** Movement Type **`122`** (Return delivery) or **`161`** (Return PO line item).
- **Q23: How do you prevent users from booking negative inventory?** Deactivate the "Negative Stock Allowed" checkbox in Plant and Storage Location customizing in `OMJ1`.
- **Q24: What is the difference between Parked and Held documents in MIRO?** A **Held** document saves incomplete data with syntax errors without balance checks. A **Parked** document passes field status validations and can be reviewed in approval workflows without posting to G/L.
- **Q25: What is the Material Ledger requirement in S/4HANA?** Material Ledger activation is 100% mandatory in S/4HANA to support parallel currencies and real-time inventory valuations.
- **Q26: What is T-Code MB51 vs MB52?** `MB51` is the Material Document History report (all movement transactions). `MB52` is the Warehouse Stock List (current stock quantities and values per storage location).
- **Q27: What is a Purchasing Value Key?** Configured in `OME1` and maintained in Material Master Purchasing view to default reminder days, over/under delivery tolerances, and shipping instructions.
- **Q28: How does S/4HANA handle output determination for Purchase Orders?** By default, S/4HANA uses **BRFplus (Business Rule Framework Plus)** instead of the legacy `NAST` condition tables.
- **Q29: What is the difference between Plant-Specific and Central Purchasing Organization?** A Central Purchasing Org negotiates enterprise-wide framework contracts for all company codes. A Plant-Specific Purchasing Org handles procurement exclusively for one plant.
- **Q30: What is movement type 501?** Receipt without purchase order directly into unrestricted stock (used for trial or sample stock).
- **Q31: What is a Physical Inventory Document?** Created in `MI01`, counted via `MI04`, and differences posted to financial G/L accounts via `MI07`.
- **Q32: What is the function of the Quota Arrangement?** T-Code `MEQ1`: Distributes total procurement requirements among multiple vendors based on defined percentage quotas.
- **Q33: Can you convert a Purchase Requisition directly into a Goods Receipt?** No. A PR is an internal demand document. It must first be converted into a Purchase Order (`ME21N`) or Scheduling Agreement before receiving goods.
- **Q34: What is the Universal Journal table in S/4HANA?** Table **`ACDOCA`**, which unifies all financial and management accounting line items into a single record.
- **Q35: How do you prepare a Functional Specification (FS) for an ABAP developer?** Identify business requirement $\rightarrow$ define trigger transaction $\rightarrow$ list exact source tables and fields $\rightarrow$ write logic flowchart $\rightarrow$ document test scenarios and error handling.
