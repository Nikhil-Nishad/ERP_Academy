## What is SAP S/4HANA Sourcing & Procurement?

In legacy SAP ECC, the procurement function was housed inside SAP MM. While ECC MM handled operational purchasing well (PRs, POs, Goods Receipts), it lacked native capabilities for supplier discovery, real-time contract compliance analytics, and centralized multi-ERP procurement.

In **SAP S/4HANA**, Sourcing and Procurement combines:
1. **Operational Procurement:** Requisitioning, purchase order automation, blanket orders, service procurement, and self-service procurement via SAP Fiori.
2. **Sourcing & Contract Management:** Request for Quotations (RFQs), automated supplier bid evaluation, contract lifecycle management, and integration with the SAP Business Network.
3. **Invoice & Payables Management:** Three-way matching, Evaluated Receipt Settlement (ERS), automated tax validation, and exception handling.
4. **Supplier Management:** Supplier performance evaluation, risk scoring, qualification questionnaires, and 360-degree vendor analytics.
5. **Central Procurement:** A multi-ERP integration hub that connects a single central S/4HANA system to multiple backend ERP systems (ECC or legacy non-SAP) to aggregate enterprise buying power.

## S/4HANA Sourcing vs Legacy ECC MM: Key Evolutions

| Capability | Legacy SAP ECC MM | Modern SAP S/4HANA Sourcing & Procurement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **User Interface** | SAP GUI menus (`ME21N`, `ME51N`) | Web-based SAP Fiori apps (Manage Purchase Orders, Create Requisitions) |
| **Approval Workflow** | Classic PO Release Strategy via Class `032` | **Flexible Workflow** for S/4HANA with multi-tier conditional rules |
| **Supplier Master** | Separate `XK01` / `MK01` tables | Unified **Business Partner (T-Code `BP`)** with CVI |
| **Material Requirements Planning** | Overnight batch MRP (`MD01`) | **MRP Live (`MD01N`)** running in minutes in-memory |
| **Inventory Documents** | Split header/item tables (`MKPF`, `MSEG`) | Single unified document table **`MATDOC`** |
| **Supplier Collaboration** | Paper, PDF attachments, manual EDI | Real-time direct cloud connection to **SAP Ariba / Business Network** |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the difference between SAP MM and SAP S/4HANA Sourcing and Procurement?
SAP S/4HANA Sourcing and Procurement is the modern evolution of SAP MM. It incorporates the in-memory `MATDOC` inventory table, real-time MRP Live, Business Partner master data, Fiori user experiences, Flexible Workflows, and native cloud integration with SAP Ariba.

### Is SAP S/4HANA Sourcing and Procurement good for mechanical and civil engineers?
Yes, exceptionally good. Engineers have an intuitive grasp of physical manufacturing parts, warehouse storage, bills of materials, and vendor supply lifecycles, making them among the fastest learners and top performers in S/4HANA procurement consulting.

### What is the official certification exam code for S/4HANA Sourcing and Procurement?
The current official exam codes are `C_TS452_2022` and `C_TS452_2023` (SAP Certified Associate - SAP S/4HANA Sourcing and Procurement). It consists of 80 multiple-choice and multi-response questions with a passing cut-off score of 65%.
