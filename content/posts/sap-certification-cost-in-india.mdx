## Exam Pattern, Format & Remote Proctoring Rules

1. **Duration:** 180 Minutes (3 Hours).
2. **Number of Questions:** Exactly 80 questions.
3. **Question Types:** 
   - Multiple Choice (Single correct answer).
   - Multiple Response (e.g., "Choose 2 of the following 4 options" or "Choose 3 correct statements"). There is **no partial marking** on multiple-response questions!
   - Scenario-Based Process Flows.
4. **Negative Marking:** **None.** There is zero negative marking for wrong answers.
5. **Exam Environment:** 
   - Conducted on your laptop via Questionmark secure browser.
   - You must have a working webcam and microphone.
   - The proctor will ask you to pan your room 360 degrees, inspect your desk, and verify your official Government Photo ID (Passport or Aadhaar card).

## Does SAP Certification Guarantee a Job in India?

Let's address the most crucial myth head-on:

$$\text{SAP Certification} \neq \text{Automatic Job Offer}$$

Every week, hiring managers at MNCs like Accenture, Capgemini, and TCS interview certified candidates who score 85% on multiple-choice dumps but stumble on fundamental enterprise questions like:
- *"How do you configure movement type 201 vs 261 in SPRO?"*
- *"Can you explain the business difference between a Purchase Requisition and a Purchase Order?"*
- *"Walk me through an OBYC automatic account determination error you encountered in your project."*

### What Certification Actually Does for You:
1. **Resume Shortlisting:** With hundreds of resumes submitted for junior and mid-level SAP openings, automated HR filters and recruitment portals (Naukri, LinkedIn) prioritize candidates holding verified SAP Global IDs (`S-User / Digital Credential`).
2. **Consulting Client Billing:** For IT services companies (TCS, Wipro, Infosys), billing a client for an offshore SAP consultant often requires presenting certified profiles in RFP bids.
3. **Credibility Validation:** If you are transitioning from non-IT or traditional finance/supply chain into SAP, an official certification proves commitment and foundational literacy.

### What Actually Wins the Job:
**Practical, hands-on configuration capability.** 
Candidates who can demonstrate real-time business scenario troubleshooting, speak fluent business process language, and execute end-to-end configuration cycles on live S/4HANA servers will beat certified candidates with zero practical fluency 100% of the time.

3. **Step 3: Practice Scenario Questions.** Build configuration scenarios yourself in the SAP GUI/Fiori interface. When you physically configure OBYC, BP roles, and pricing procedures, theoretical exam questions become intuitive.
4. **Step 4: Book via CER006.** Leverage the multi-attempt safety net to take the exam without paralyzing test anxiety.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I take the SAP certification exam without enrolling in an authorized partner institute?
Yes. SAP now allows candidates to purchase the SAP Certification Hub subscription (`CER001` or `CER006`) directly from the official SAP Training Shop (`training.sap.com`) and appear for remotely proctored exams from home without paying mandatory multi-lakh institute fees.

### What is the validity of an SAP Global Certification?
SAP certifications remain valid for several years as long as the underlying solution version is in mainstream support. In SAP S/4HANA Cloud, SAP has introduced "Stay Certified" micro-assessments, allowing certified professionals to maintain active status by completing short periodic knowledge checks.

### How much does the SAP CER006 voucher cost in Indian Rupees?
As of 2025–2026, the CER006 voucher costs approximately ₹42,000 to ₹46,000 plus 18% GST (totaling ~₹49,500 to ₹54,000). It includes up to 6 exam attempts valid across a 12-month period.

### Which SAP module certification has the highest demand in India?
SAP S/4HANA Sourcing and Procurement (`C_TS452` / SAP MM), SAP S/4HANA Financial Accounting (`C_TS4FI` / SAP FICO), and SAP ABAP Cloud / HANA Technology have consistently ranked as the most hired and highest-paying modules across Indian IT service providers and Global Capability Centers (GCCs).
